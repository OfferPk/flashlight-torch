Battery-friendly notes

  • LED torch uses less power than max screen brightness for long sessions.
  • Lower strobe Hz and turn off on pause (defaults on) save battery.
  • Screen Wake Lock keeps the display on only while light is active.
  • No location, mic, storage, or contacts permissions.
