Privacy Policy

Offer Torch / Flashlight · com.offerpk.flashlight · Local document (v1.0.0-complete)

Summary

This app is designed to work offline. It does not require an account. Core flashlight / screen-light features do not send your personal data to OfferPk servers.

Permissions explained

  • Camera / flashlight (optional): Used only to control the rear LED torch via Android CameraManager.setTorchMode (and strength APIs on Android 13+). We prefer torch control without opening a camera capture preview. Declared with android.hardware.camera / android.hardware.camera.flash as required="false" so the app still installs on devices without a flash (Screen Light remains available).
  • Not used: location, microphone, storage/photos library, contacts, phone.

Data stored on device

Settings (theme, strobe speed, default-on, ad unlock stubs, epilepsy acknowledgement) are stored in on-device localStorage / app preferences. They stay on your device unless you clear app data.

Ads (stub / optional)

Banner placeholders appear only on Settings and Themes — never delaying Torch ON. Rewarded stubs can unlock extra screen-light colors or a one-time remove-ads preference. When real AdMob (or similar) is wired, that SDK’s privacy disclosures and consent flows apply; this local policy will be updated accordingly before Play production with live ads.

Children

Not directed at children under 13. Strobe includes a photosensitive epilepsy warning.
